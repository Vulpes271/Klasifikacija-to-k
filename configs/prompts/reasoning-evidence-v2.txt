fixed: the supplied text identifies a structural building element or establishes
that an element is permanently installed or integrated into the building.
Something remains fixed even if it could be removed by dismantling, unbolting or
demolishing its installation. Theoretical removability does not make it movable.

movable: an object that is normally relocated, carried, pushed or transported
during ordinary use, when this is supported by its described identity and context.

establishes that relationship. Classify its position by the movement of that whole,
not by whether the component is attached to it. Do not invent a part-whole link
from proximity, similar names or overlapping coordinates alone.

uncertain: the supplied identity, context or mounting evidence is insufficient,
ambiguous or conflicting. For shelves, signs and other equipment without enough
information about installation or normal relocation, use uncertain. Neither size,
standing still nor proximity to a wall establishes permanent installation.
